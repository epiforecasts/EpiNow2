Reverse pass of the convolution on doubles.

Accumulates xbar_{1:m} += w_d zbar_{d+1:d+m} and wbar_d += zbar_{d+1:d+m}^T x_{1:m}, writing wbar_d to ybar_{D - d}.

Parameters

yReversed PMF of length D.
zbarAdjoint of the output, of length len.
xbarAdjoint of x, of length n, accumulated into. May be null.
ybarAdjoint of y, of length D, accumulated into. May be null.

Sums used by the Newton step and the gradient.

Computes S0 = sum_k p_k exp(-r k) and S1 = sum_k k p_k exp(-r k), with p_k = g[G - 1 - k].
